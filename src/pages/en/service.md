## Six approaches

### Building laboratories in space

We use the extreme environment of space to nurture questions and technologies that are difficult to create on Earth.

### Inventing life in space

We imagine life in space and invent systems that enrich the future of everyday life.

### Looking back at Earth from space

We look at Earth from space and create new perspectives on the environment and society.

### Supporting the next generation of challenges

Using space as a theme, we create places where the next generation can ask their own questions and take on challenges.
